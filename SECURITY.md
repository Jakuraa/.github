# Security Policy

Jakuraa Commercial Private Limited prioritizes the security and cryptographic integrity of our autonomous agent infrastructure, machine commerce rails, and smart contracts.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability or potential threat in any Jakuraa system, smart contract, or open-source library, please report it immediately to our security response team:

**Email:** [security@jakuraa.com](mailto:security@jakuraa.com)

Please include:
- A description of the issue and potential impact.
- Detailed reproduction steps, proof of concept (PoC), or code snippet.
- The repository, branch, commit, contract address, or live endpoint affected.

### Response Commitment
- **Initial Response:** Within 24 business hours.
- **Triage & Status Update:** Within 3 business days.
- **Coordination:** We request that you adhere to coordinated vulnerability disclosure principles and do not disclose vulnerabilities publicly until a remediated fix has been published.

## Out of Scope
- Denial of Service (DoS / DDoS) attacks against public infrastructure endpoints.
- Social engineering, phishing, or physical attacks against employees or facilities.
- Issues in third-party broker APIs or external protocols outside our control.
